Use **Mellox AI** as the public company and product name, including the deck,
social profile display names and payment branding. Social usernames must follow
each platform's formatting rules; keep existing verified URLs until the accounts
have actually been renamed.

The public website keeps founder biographies, portraits and personal profile
links off the About page, company structured data, sitemap and AI-readable site
exports. Retired founder profile URLs redirect to `/about`.

The billing catalog is the price source of truth. Scale costs **$1,199/month**
when billed monthly or **$11,990/year** when billed annually. Its annual monthly
equivalent is approximately **$999.17**, not a monthly subscription price. The
pricing page opens with monthly billing and labels annual equivalents explicitly.
