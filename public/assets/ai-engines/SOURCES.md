These SVG marks are local copies of the corresponding Simple Icons v16 assets:

- [Perplexity](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@v16/icons/perplexity.svg)
- [DeepSeek](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@v16/icons/deepseek.svg)

- [Poe](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@v16/icons/poe.svg)

The Copilot, Grok, and Meta AI vectors come from [Lobe Icons v1.95.1](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons). The Apple Intelligence icon comes from [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/apple-intelligence/) and was resized to 128 × 128 pixels for this interface.

Only the root fill color was added to the Simple Icons marks. Grok's monochrome fill was set explicitly for theme handling. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini use the existing inline marks in `src/components/brand/BrandLogo.tsx`.
