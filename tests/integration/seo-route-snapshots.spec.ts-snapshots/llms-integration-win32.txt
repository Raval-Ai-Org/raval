Teams chat with Mellox to run AEO/GEO audits, generate on-brand content, plan calendars, coordinate approvals and manage many client brands at once. Strategy and execution live in the same product, grounded in each brand's DNA. ## Pages - [Studio](/app): Chat-first workspace where Mellox plans, creates and optimizes for the active brand. - [Agency HQ](/agency): Manage all clients at once — approvals, schedules, activity and combined analytics. - [Clients](/projects): Every brand in one place with Brand DNA, memory and recent activity. - [AI Search Visibility (AEO / GEO)](/app): Site-wide scans with 60+ checks that show how brands appear inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. - [Content](/app): On-brand articles, briefs, social and landing copy grounded in Brand DNA. - [Analytics](/app): Unified traffic, AI citations and pipeline insights. ## Optional - [Sign in](/login) - [Create account](/signup)
